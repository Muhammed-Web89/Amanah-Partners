The most important step in planning to study abroad is choosing the country that fits your goals and budget. A-Levels open the door to universities across the world. Below are the most popular destinations and what each one offers.

## United Kingdom

The home of the British education system. Applications go through UCAS. Universities decide mainly on A-Level grades (or predicted grades), and they also read the personal statement and the teacher’s reference.

With A-Levels you can start without a foundation year. In England a bachelor’s degree usually takes 3 years.

## USA

US universities review applications holistically through the Common App: grades, test scores, extracurricular activities and essays are read together.

A-Levels show academic depth. Combined with a strong SAT score and extracurricular activities, they improve the chances of admission to strong universities and of receiving a scholarship.

## Germany and Italy

The main choice for students looking for high-quality, relatively affordable education in Europe. With only a local school certificate, these countries usually require a Studienkolleg in Germany first, or one or two years of university study in Uzbekistan.

Three A-Level subjects in the right combination let you apply directly to a bachelor’s degree in Germany and Italy. That is why subjects are chosen with the target country in mind from the start.

## Canada and the Netherlands

In Canada, leading universities such as Toronto and McGill accept A-Level students. Some universities also give first-year credit for certain A-Level subjects.

The Netherlands is a strong European alternative, with many English-taught bachelor’s programmes and relatively affordable tuition.
