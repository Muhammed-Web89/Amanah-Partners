One of the things students and parents most often confuse when applying to universities abroad is the exams. Working out early which exam you need saves both time and money.

## IELTS and TOEFL: language exams

These exams do not test maths, logic or subject knowledge. They only confirm your English skills — speaking, listening, reading and writing — against an international standard.

- **When are they needed?** Applicants whose first language is not English are usually asked for a language certificate when applying to an English-taught programme, whether in the UK, the USA or Europe. Some universities make exceptions in certain cases, so the requirement is checked for each university.
- **For the visa:** a student visa also often requires proof of your English level.

## SAT: the academic test

The SAT is a standardised test of critical reading and writing in English, and of maths.

- **When is it needed?** If you apply to US universities, many of them require the SAT, and a strong score clearly strengthens the application and the chances of a scholarship.
- **In Europe:** some competitive courses, such as certain economics and business programmes in Italy, accept an SAT score instead of their own entrance test.

## In short

IELTS or TOEFL is needed by almost everyone applying to an English-taught programme. The SAT is mainly for students aiming at the USA, or who want to make their application academically stronger.
