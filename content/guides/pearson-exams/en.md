## Online lessons, exams in person

You can prepare for Pearson Edexcel International A-Levels (IAL) online. The exams, however, are not taken at home or online: they are sat on paper at a physical Pearson-approved centre.

## What is a private candidate?

A student whose own school does not run Pearson exams sits them as a **private candidate**. They register through an approved centre. Each centre decides for itself whether to accept private candidates.

## When are the exam series?

- **International A-Level:** October, January, May–June.
- **International GCSE:** November, May–June.

Not every subject and unit is available in every series. Exact dates are published before each series.

## Registration steps

1. Subject and unit codes and the target series are set.
2. A suitable centre is chosen and confirms it accepts private candidates.
3. Registration is completed before the final deadline.
4. Exam day: address, photo ID and timings.
5. Results, a resit plan if needed, and the certificate.

## Improving a grade

Every IAL subject except Law is modular. To improve a grade you resit only the unit you need, not the whole subject.
